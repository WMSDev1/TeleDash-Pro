Calendar

Paste your calendar's secret address in iCal format (Google: Settings → your calendar → "Secret address in iCal format"; iCloud: share calendar → Public Calendar link). It is stored on the TV only and never shown again.

Google sign-in is not available in this build of TeleDash Pro. Use an iCal link instead, or install a release build.

Google may say it hasn't verified TeleDash Pro yet. Tap Advanced, then Go to TeleDash Pro. TeleDash Pro only reads your calendars, and the sign-in is kept on this TV.
