TeleDash Pro

An open-source home screen for rooted LG webOS TVs, installed through Homebrew Channel.

Screenshot coming soon

What it does

  • Big tiles for the apps you actually use, ordered by how often you open them.
  • Clock, weather, market indexes, today's calendar and countdowns at a glance.
  • Optional: make the remote's Home button open TeleDash Pro instead of LG's launcher.
  • Set it up from your phone by scanning a QR code on the TV. No account, no cloud service.

Add it to Homebrew Channel

  1. On the TV, open Homebrew Channel and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Add repository and enter this address: https://<owner>.github.io/<repo>/repo/apps.json
  3. Go back to the app list, open TeleDash Pro and choose Install.
  4. Launch it. On first start it offers to install a small helper (needs root) so you can set it up from your phone and make Home open TeleDash Pro.

Homebrew Channel needs a rooted TV. The dashboard itself also works without the helper, with built-in defaults.
