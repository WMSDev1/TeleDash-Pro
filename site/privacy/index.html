Privacy policy

TeleDash Pro is an open-source home screen for LG webOS TVs. It runs entirely on your TV. There is no TeleDash server, no account, and no analytics or tracking.

Effective 17 November 2026.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, TeleDash Pro asks Google for read-only access to your calendars (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly). It cannot create, change or delete anything.

  • What it reads: the list of your calendars (so you can choose which to show) and, for the calendars you choose, the title, start time and end time of events from today for the next few days.
  • What it does with it: shows those events on your TV. Nothing else.
  • Where it goes: your TV fetches the events directly from Google. They are kept in the TV's memory for a few minutes and are not saved to disk. They are never sent to the TeleDash Pro developers or to anyone else.
  • Sign-in tokens that Google issues are stored only on your TV, in a file readable only by the TV's administrator (root) account.
  • Sign-in return page: after you sign in on your phone, Google sends your phone to a page on this site, which passes the result straight to your TV on your home network. The page does not store or transmit it anywhere else. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's own privacy statement.

TeleDash Pro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Removing access and deleting data

  • On the TV's setup page, Disconnect Google deletes the sign-in tokens from the TV.
  • You can revoke TeleDash Pro's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling TeleDash Pro and its helper removes everything it stored on the TV.

Other data

  • Settings and keys you enter on the setup page stay on your TV.
  • Your location, if you set one for weather, is rounded to about 10 km before it is saved.
  • App usage (which TV app was open, and when) is recorded on the TV only, to order the tiles. It never leaves the TV.
  • The TV contacts only the services you turn on: Open-Meteo (weather and air quality), the US National Weather Service (warnings), a public stock-quote service (market strip), your calendar provider, your data-feed address, and this site (update checks). Each of them sees your home's internet address when asked for data.
