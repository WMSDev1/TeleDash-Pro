TeleDash Pro privacy policy

This policy explains what data the TeleDash Pro app accesses, including Google user data, how it is used, stored and shared, and how you can delete it.

Effective 17 November 2026.

Who we are

TeleDash Pro is a free, open-source home screen app for LG webOS televisions. It runs entirely on your own TV. The developers run no server for it: there is no TeleDash Pro account, and no data from the app is sent to the developers.

Google user data the app accesses

Only if you choose to connect Google Calendar, TeleDash Pro asks Google for one permission, read-only access to your calendars (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly). It cannot create, change or delete anything. With it, the app reads:

  • the list of your calendars and their names, so you can choose which ones to show;
  • for the calendars you choose, each event's title, start time and end time, for today and the next few days.

It does not read attendees, descriptions, locations, attachments, contacts, email or any other Google data, and it does not access your Google account profile.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used for one purpose: to display your upcoming events on your own TV screen. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

How the data is stored and protected

  • Your TV requests events directly from Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Events are kept only in the TV's memory, for up to a few minutes, so the screen can show them. They are not written to disk.
  • The sign-in tokens that Google issues are stored only on your TV, in a file readable only by the TV's administrator (root) account. They are never sent to your phone, to the developers, or anywhere other than Google.
  • After you sign in on your phone, Google returns your phone to a page on this website (/oauth/). That page runs in your browser and passes the one-time sign-in code straight to your TV on your home network. It does not store or send it anywhere else.

Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone. The developers never receive it. It is not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

TeleDash Pro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Calendar events are discarded from memory within minutes and whenever the TV restarts.
  • Sign-in tokens stay on the TV until you remove them. Disconnect Google on the TV's setup page deletes them immediately.
  • You can revoke TeleDash Pro's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the TV can no longer read your calendar.
  • Uninstalling TeleDash Pro and its helper deletes everything the app stored on the TV.

Other data

  • Settings and keys you enter on the TV's setup page stay on your TV.
  • Your location, if you set one for weather, is rounded to about 10 km before it is saved on the TV.
  • App usage (which TV app was open, and when) is recorded on the TV only, to order the tiles. It never leaves the TV.
  • The TV contacts only the services you turn on: Open-Meteo (weather and air quality), the US National Weather Service (warnings), a public stock-quote service (market strip), your calendar provider, your data-feed address, and this website (update checks). Each of them sees your home's internet address when asked for data.
  • This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's own privacy statement. The site has no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Children

TeleDash Pro is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Changes are published on this page with a new effective date. The full history is public in the project's source repository.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository. Security problems: use the repository's private vulnerability reporting (Security tab).
